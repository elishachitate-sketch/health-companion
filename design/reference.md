# Visual reference notes

Reference image: `/home/elisha/Downloads/heeee.jpeg`

The image is inspiration only. It does not define clinical requirements, medical claims, or implementation instructions.

## Patterns to adapt

- Calm white and pale-blue surfaces with blue as the primary action color.
- Conversational health assistant as the main entry point.
- Small, readable insight cards instead of dense dashboards.
- Voice interaction for hands-free check-ins.
- Simple charts for trends such as sleep, activity, and wellbeing.
- Clear next actions below each insight.
- Visible distinction between user-reported information, device measurements, and AI interpretation.

## Patterns to improve for our product

- Replace diagnostic-sounding language with cautious, plain-language observations.
- Show confidence and limitations when AI summarizes a pattern.
- Add consent, privacy, and sharing controls near sensitive health information.
- Include an obvious human-support path for red flags.
- Design for accessibility, low bandwidth, and users who do not own health wearables.

## Initial screen direction

1. **Today** — wellbeing check-in, current status, and one suggested action.
2. **Chat** — supportive conversation with source-aware summaries.
3. **Insights** — weekly patterns with explanations and uncertainty.
4. **Support** — trusted contacts, care navigation, and urgent-help guidance.
5. **Privacy** — consent, data controls, export, and deletion.
