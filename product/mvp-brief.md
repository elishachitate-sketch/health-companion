# Health Companion MVP brief

## Working concept

Kuda+Lumi Health Companion is a mobile-first wellbeing support and early-triage experience. It helps a person notice changes in mood, stress, sleep, and daily functioning, then choose a safe next step.

## Intended users

Adults who want a private, low-friction way to reflect on their wellbeing and decide whether self-care, a trusted person, primary care, or urgent support is appropriate.

## What it does

- Collects consented daily check-ins.
- Detects patterns and risk signals in the supplied information.
- Explains observations in plain language.
- Offers small, practical actions.
- Identifies red flags and presents escalation guidance.
- Keeps the user in control of history, sharing, and deletion.

## What it does not do

- Diagnose mental or physical health conditions.
- Replace a clinician or emergency service.
- Infer sensitive attributes that the user did not provide.
- Present uncertain model output as fact.

## First demo journey

The demo will show a new user completing a two-minute check-in, receiving a transparent wellbeing summary, choosing an action, and seeing how a simulated red-flag response routes them toward human support.

## Pilot hypothesis

A conversational check-in with transparent boundaries can improve early recognition of wellbeing concerns and reduce friction to appropriate support, while maintaining user trust through consent, privacy controls, and human escalation.
